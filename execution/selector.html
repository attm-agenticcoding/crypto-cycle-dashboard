Each candidate is scored on the same 26 completed weeks. Its weekly cost difference from the raw minimum determines its own one-standard-error band, with a 0.25 bp floor. Within that band, the nearest prior parameter combination is retained.

This is a stability heuristic, not a significance test or proof of equal performance. The model values unfilled quantity at the next reference and does not simulate spreads, fees, queue position or market impact. Listed instruments use spot proxies.
