What the calculator is solving

Market spacing, dynamic sizing, and the deadline pace guardrail

Market layer. The updater searches rolling lookback × first offset × spacing on the selected instrument's registered proxy, —. Every historical reference is the close of the 09:35 ET bar; eligible fills begin at 09:36. Candidate ladders are compared by walk-forward weekly implementation shortfall. A one-standard-error plateau and the prior published parameter regularize the result.

Sizing layer. Once spacing is fixed, shares are a control output: remaining target shares divided by remaining sessions and expected rung hits. Buy targets are entered in currency; sell targets are entered in shares and capped at holdings after accounting for existing sell orders. Buy limits round down and passive sell limits round up to cents.

Deadline layer. Supplying total capital remaining activates an explicit schedule guardrail. The even-pace floor is total remaining × eligible sessions in the current window ÷ sessions to the deadline; the current window ends at the earlier of week-end or the deadline. The effective target is the larger of that floor and the entered weekly target, capped at total remaining. If the floor causes a pace lift, market distances tighten by 1 ÷ √(pace lift), bounded between 0.40× and 1.00×. This rule is deliberately monotone: moving the deadline closer can never reduce the target or widen the ladder.

Sell scaling. Sell fits use the largest upward move after the reference, independently of buy drawdowns. The objective minimizes sale shortfall: reference value minus sale proceeds, with unfilled weekly shares valued at the next reference. That terminal valuation is a backtest assumption. Expected hits are proxy price touches, not guaranteed fills; the proxy does not capture the listed instrument's bid–ask spread, queue, partial fills or fees.

Sale priority. Price seeking keeps a passive ladder. Finish by deadline adds a manual closeout 15 minutes before the final eligible session closes: recalculate with updated fills, holdings, outstanding orders and a fresh best bid. It generates a sell limit at that bid, rounded down to cents. Check the broker's allowed tick before placing it. A moving quote can leave it unfilled. The deadline and closeout rules are explicit overlays, not separately fitted optima. Nothing on this page submits or cancels broker orders. See the NYSE session calendar.
