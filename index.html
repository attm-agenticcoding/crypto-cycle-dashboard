Legacy policy simulator · research reference, not a validated allocation recommendation

The legacy policy uses unvalidated valuation assumptions, and its historical utility result does not certify today's allocation. Enter the asset's total pool and how much is already deployed—whether old or newly contributed. Below full policy, the controller buys one-third of the remaining full-policy shortfall and reassesses after the next weekly close, capped at 10% of the total pool per week. Raw remains a structural posterior reference rather than a stopping target. At full it holds; above full it stops adding but does not force a sale. Top/distribution has priority and is the only layer allowed to trim. Minimum ticket defaults to $0 and the no-trade band defaults to 1.0pp. Runs entirely in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
